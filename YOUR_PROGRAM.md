# Your Momentum Program

## Objective

Lose body fat while preserving or building useful strength, improving cardiovascular fitness, and keeping the routine realistic during busy workweeks.

## Weekly schedule

| Day | Session | Approximate time |
|---|---|---:|
| Monday | Full Body A + incline treadmill walk | 40–45 min |
| Tuesday | Peloton easy endurance | 30–45 min by phase |
| Wednesday | Full Body B + easy treadmill walk | 40–45 min |
| Thursday | Peloton controlled intervals | 26–30 min by phase |
| Friday | Optional recovery walk or full rest | 20–30 min |
| Saturday | Full Body C + easy Peloton walk | 45–50 min |
| Sunday | Full rest | — |

## Strength sessions

### Monday — Full Body A

1. Goblet squat — 3 × 10–15
2. Dumbbell floor press — 3 × 10–15
3. One-arm dumbbell row — 3 × 10–15 per side
4. Dumbbell Romanian deadlift — 3 × 10–15
5. Ab roller — 2 × 5–10 controlled reps
6. Incline treadmill walk — 8–10 minutes

### Wednesday — Full Body B

1. Reverse lunge — 3 × 8–12 per leg
2. Dumbbell shoulder press — 3 × 8–12
3. One-arm dumbbell row — 3 × 10–15 per side
4. Dumbbell glute bridge — 3 × 12–20
5. Dumbbell curl — 2 × 10–15
6. Front plank — 2 × 30–45 seconds
7. Easy treadmill walk — 5–10 minutes

### Saturday — Full Body C

1. Dumbbell front squat — 3 × 10–15
2. Incline push-up — 3 sets, stopping about two reps before failure
3. Dumbbell Romanian deadlift — 3 × 12–15
4. One-arm dumbbell row — 3 × 12–15 per side
5. Dumbbell lateral raise — 2 × 12–20
6. Ab roller — 2 × 5–10
7. Peloton low-impact walk — 10–15 minutes

### Strength progression

Finish most sets with about two technically clean repetitions still available. Reach the top of the prescribed repetition range on every set before increasing the dumbbell weight.

The adjustable dumbbells stop at 25 pounds. Once 25 pounds is no longer challenging, progress by using slower lowering phases, pauses, one-and-a-half repetitions, unilateral variations, or more repetitions rather than adding unavailable weight.

## Eight-week cardio progression

| Weeks | Tuesday endurance | Thursday intervals |
|---|---:|---|
| 1–2 | 30 min at effort 5–6/10 | 5-min warmup; 8 × 30 sec hard / 90 sec easy; 5-min cooldown |
| 3–4 | 35 min at effort 5–6/10 | 5-min warmup; 8 × 45 sec hard / 75 sec easy; 5-min cooldown |
| 5–6 | 40 min at effort 5–6/10 | 5-min warmup; 8 × 60 sec hard / 60 sec easy; 5-min cooldown |
| 7–8 | 45 min at effort 5–6/10 | 5-min warmup; 10 × 60 sec hard / 60 sec easy; 5-min cooldown |

A hard interval is approximately 8/10 effort—not an all-out sprint. The app automatically changes the targets as each program week begins.

## 10-Minute Minimum

When the planned session is not realistic, complete either:

- Any 10-minute Peloton class, or
- A 10-minute treadmill walk

Use **Workout → Start 10 min**. It protects the habit and counts as the day's workout.

## Starting targets

- Protein: 150 grams per day, editable under Coach > App and plan settings
- Water: 80 ounces per day, editable under Coach > App and plan settings
- Meal and workout times: editable under Coach > App and plan settings

The protein target is intentionally a starting placeholder until body-size and related information are used to personalize it further.

## Safety

Use controlled technique and stop an exercise that causes sharp pain, chest pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath. This routine is a general fitness plan and does not replace individualized medical or nutrition advice.
